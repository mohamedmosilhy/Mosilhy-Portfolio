## Overview

Nova is an Arabic-first commerce platform for physical and digital products. It combines a configurable customer storefront with administration workflows for catalog, inventory, orders, payments, shipping, content, analytics, themes, landing pages, and integrations.

<ProjectGallery />

## Features

- Physical and digital products with variants, attributes, inventory, brands, categories, and reviews.
- Quantity offers, customer-configurable bundles, cross-sells, cart, checkout, and country- and city-aware shipping.
- Cash on Delivery and administrator-defined proof-based payment methods.
- Independent order and payment lifecycles with transaction snapshots.
- Administration for analytics, catalog, orders, payments, moderation, settings, content, and themes.
- Landing pages, SEO fields, tracking pixels, and WhatsApp and n8n integration configuration.

## Architecture

The Next.js App Router application is a feature-based modular monolith. Route handlers and server-rendered routes delegate to feature services, validation schemas, repositories and queries, and shared infrastructure. Prisma maps the PostgreSQL domains and migrations. Auth.js credentials and JWT sessions protect administration roles, while Zustand and TanStack Query have separate client-state and server-state responsibilities.
