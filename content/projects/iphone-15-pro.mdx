## Overview

This educational recreation of Apple’s iPhone 15 Pro product page combines responsive product media, scroll-based narrative sections, a timed highlights carousel, and a configurable WebGL phone model. It does not implement product or commerce APIs.

<ProjectGallery />

## Features

- Responsive hero video with desktop and mobile sources.
- A four-item highlights carousel with playback, replay, progress, and timing controls.
- An interactive 3D phone with four color options and two model sizes.
- Scroll-triggered feature videos, typography, and A17 presentation.
- Sentry error, performance, and session-replay integration.

## Architecture

React section components divide the page into navigation, hero, highlights, model, feature, chip, and footer areas. Centralized constants provide product variants and page content. Animation utilities coordinate GSAP timelines, while React Three Fiber and Drei own the Three.js scene.
